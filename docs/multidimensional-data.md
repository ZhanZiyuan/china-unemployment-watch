# Multidimensional research data

All published estimates are sourced from the [National Bureau of Statistics of China](https://www.stats.gov.cn/). The cited releases remain subject to the [NBS reuse and attribution terms](https://www.stats.gov.cn/wzgl/202302/t20230217_1912857.html). The data registry defines per-series frequency, units, population, ranges, publication lag and adjustment.

| Source and scope | Verified coverage in the initial snapshot | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Construction and services PMI | 46 months per series, January 2023–October 2026 | Activity, new orders and employment per sector. Published diffusion points, source seasonally adjusted. |
| Real estate development and sales | 39 reporting periods per series, January–February 2023 to January–October 2026 | Investment and sales amounts and developer funds in 100 million CNY. Starts, construction area, completions and sales area in 10,000 m². Each has an original cumulative level and a comparable-basis cumulative year-over-year rate as published. The period begins in January. |
| Migrant-worker survey | Three annual observations per series, 2023–2025 | Total, out-of-town, local, living in urban areas at year-end, and interprovincial counts in 10,000 persons. Construction and manufacturing shares use the survey population. Monthly wage is the survey's annual average in CNY per month. |
| National statistical communiqué | Three annual observations per series, 2023–2025 | Construction sector value added and profit of qualified general and specialist construction contractors, in 100 million CNY. The latter series covers the qualified enterprise population. |

January and February property statistics are one reported interval. December 2023, 2024 and 2025 are missing in all 14 real-estate series. The dated official year-end reports exist, but after recognizing their distinct year-over-year header the targeted retrieval returned access challenge pages. The available periods were retained. No month-only values or growth rates were derived from totals. Construction area covers work during the reporting interval and is unsuitable for flow differencing without additional assumptions. The official comparable-basis rate can use a revised denominator and should not be recomputed from two published absolute levels.

The annual construction yearbook identifies qualified enterprise tables, including workers and contracts. The reviewed yearbook tables are image publications and lack verified dated vintages for the requested national series. Headcount, gross output and newly signed contract value remain unconnected. Construction value added and qualified-enterprise profit measure separate quantities. Changes in migrant-worker counts cannot establish how many people became unemployed.

The research observation contract is separate from the baseline 1.0.0 contract. Recorded URLs, dates, acquisition timestamps and SHA-256 hashes point to gzip-compressed original HTML under `data/raw/` and matching response metadata. `data/metadata/research-releases.json` maps the new release bodies to their archive titles. The research publisher replays both ledgers; public values keep the native reporting intervals and coverage by series. The baseline manifest, observations, model calibration and archived benchmark are independent of the research files.
